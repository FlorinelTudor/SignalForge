# Research Brief: Pilot/Controller Glossary (10/12/2017)

## Document profile
- Title: Pilot/Controller Glossary
- Date in document header: 10/12/17
- Pages: 120
- Primary purpose: establish common operational ATC phraseology and term definitions.

## Key findings relevant to your audio work
- "CLEARED FOR TAKEOFF" is explicitly defined as ATC authorization for departure, based on known traffic and airport conditions.
- "WAKE TURBULENCE" is defined broadly to include vortices and jet/prop/rotor wash effects in air and on ground.
- "AIRCRAFT CLASSES" defines wake categories (Super, Heavy, Large, Small); "Heavy" starts at 300,000 lbs MTOW.
- "READ BACK" is explicitly defined as: repeat the controller message back.
- "TRAFFIC PATTERN" explicitly defines "upwind leg" as parallel to runway in landing direction.

## Why this matters for your transcription
- Your ATC clips containing "caution wake turbulence" and "runway XX cleared for takeoff" align strongly with glossary-standard phraseology.
- "Heavy Airbus" usage is consistent with wake-turbulence category language.
- Pilot readback structure in your clips is consistent with glossary communication intent (read back clearances/instructions).
